Racoon Holiday

Masked little bandits with bandit-striped tails, Trudging through dust on the desert-hot trails. We left all the trash cans of home far behind, To see what sweet wonders the West had in kind. We stood on the rim of the canyon so vast, Where shadow and sunlight danced purple and fast. We scampered past saguaros reaching up high, Like giant green prickly arms touching the sky. Through red rocks of Sedona and canyons of red, We wandered all day till we tumbled to bed. With dust on our paws and the sun in our eyes, Arizona's the home of the best raccoon skies!
